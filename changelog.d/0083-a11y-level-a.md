[BugFixes: Accessibility]
- The theme button's spoken name now starts with the word it shows
  (Light, Dark or System), so speech users can say what they see.
- Checkboxes no longer hold a hidden second checkbox that screen
  readers could land on.
- The log viewer (application log stream and others) can be scrolled
  from the keyboard: Tab reaches it and the arrow keys scroll it.
- Pages that scroll but have no buttons or links (build packs, feature
  flags, quotas) can be scrolled from the keyboard: Tab reaches the
  page content and the arrow keys scroll it.
