[Accessibility: 2.4.3 Focus Order]
- On a narrow window or at high zoom, the closed side menu no longer
  takes keyboard focus or screen reader attention. Tab used to move
  through its links while they were off screen, with no visible focus.
- Opening the side menu with Toggle menu moves focus into it. Escape
  or its "<" button closes it and returns focus to Toggle menu;
  choosing a link closes it and moves focus to the new page.

[Accessibility: 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum)]
- While the side menu is open on a narrow window, Escape closes it from
  anywhere on the page, so a control it covers shows again without
  moving focus.

[Accessibility: 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value]
- The Toggle menu button says whether the side menu is open.
