[BugFixes]
- Text throughout the console now meets the 4.5:1 contrast minimum of
  WCAG 1.4.3 in light and dark mode. The default blue is a deeper
  #2563eb, so links in tables, active tabs and primary buttons read
  clearly; dark mode keeps its lighter blue and draws dark text on
  primary buttons. Placeholder text in inputs and dropdowns, text in the
  status colors (such as an application's state), the About page's
  admin badge, and the dimmed labels on the quota pages are darker.
  Operators who configure their own brand colors are unaffected.
